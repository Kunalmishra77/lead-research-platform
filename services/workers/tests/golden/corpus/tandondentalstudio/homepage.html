Dr. Avi Tandon
  

  

  
Dr. Avi Tandon is a dedicated dental professional with keen interest in Aesthetic Dentistry. Driven by a passion for enhancing smiles, Dr. Tandon pursued his postgraduation in Aesthetic and Cosmetic Dentistry from Sri Ramachandra Dental College, Chennai—ranked among the top 7 dental colleges in India. Formerly associated with Maulana Azad Institute of Dental Sciences, the top dental college in India, Dr. Tandon brings clinical excellence and a refined aesthetic sense to every case he handles .He is also trained in advanced digital and study model mockups, offering patients a unique “Test Drive” experience—allowing them to preview their smile before the actual treatment. His core philosophy is simple: Better Smiles, Happier Lives.
