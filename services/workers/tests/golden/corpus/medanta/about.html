About Medanta

Medanta, one of the country’s largest multi-speciality tertiary care providers, was founded by Dr. Naresh Trehan, a world-renowned cardiovascular and cardiothoracic surgeon, with the mission to deliver advanced yet affordable medical services to patients. Ranked as the #1 Hospital in India in 2026, Medanta Gurugram has been recognized among the World’s Best Hospitals by Newsweek for seven consecutive years (2020–2026). It is the only Indian private hospital to be consistently counted among the world’s top 150 medical facilities, setting benchmarks in clinical excellence, patient care, and medical innovation.
