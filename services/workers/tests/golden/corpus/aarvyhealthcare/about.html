Our Journey

The Beginning

2004

Laying the Foundation

[prose removed for the corpus]

2016

A Corporate Healthcare Entity

In July 2016, Aarvy Healthcare was officially incorporated as Aarvy Healthcare Pvt. Ltd. and entered the organised multi-speciality healthcare landscape as a major entity in Sector 90, Gurgaon.

2022

The Flagship Takes Shape

In January 2022, Aarvy Healthcare became a multi-speciality hospital in Sector 90, Gurgaon. With a 103-bedded facility, it emerged as a super-speciality healthcare institution serving the growing communities of New Gurugram.

2022

Healthcare Meets Education

In September 2022, Aarvy Healthcare joined hands with Amity University Haryana for health and education initiatives, strengthening the connection between medical practice, learning and knowledge development.

2023

Expanding Cancer Care

In May 2023, Aarvy Healthcare collaborated with American Oncology Institute (AOI) to provide comprehensive cancer care supported by advanced technology and specialised clinical expertise.
