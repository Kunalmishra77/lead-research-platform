The Evolution of TRINORVA ASCENDANT

TRINORVA ASCENDANT was established in 2025 in downtown Gurugram, Haryana, by a group of fitness enthusiasts who envisioned a space where health and community intertwine. Over the years, we’ve expanded our offerings, integrating diverse fitness programs to cater to our members’ unique goals. With a commitment to excellence, we’ve rapidly become a trusted destination for those seeking a transformative fitness experience.
